# Code of conduct

Trailwarden is a small project and wants to stay a pleasant place to report a
bug, propose a rule or send a fix.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people writing here are doing it in
  their spare time, and many in a second language.
- Talk about the code, the rules and the behavior, not about the person.
- Accept that a "no" to a feature is about the tool's scope, and say so when you
  give one.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background
  or experience.
- Posting someone else's private information — including paths, names or
  transcripts taken from their machine — without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's private reporting (`Security` → `Report a vulnerability`) or
contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and handled confidentially. A contributor who breaks these
rules may get a warning, have comments removed, or be blocked from the project.
